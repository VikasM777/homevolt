🏠 HomeVolt Smart Home Energy Monitor

Made for Indian homes · ₹ · kWh · PM Surya Ghar

Know where every rupee of your electricity bill goes.

HomeVolt turns your appliance list into a live energy dashboard, a bill forecast, savings ideas and a rooftop-solar payback estimate. Everything runs in your browser and is saved on your device.

How it works

1. Review the sample appliances or enter yours. 2. Set your tariff in Settings. 3. Explore savings and solar. 4. Print your report.
